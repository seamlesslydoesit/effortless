# Star Tile Studio

Make seamless square tiles with Arabic (Islamic) star patterns, coloured like
Portuguese azulejos and Mexican Talavera tiles.

**How to use:** open `index.html` in any web browser (double-click it).

- Pick a ready-made design, or press **Surprise me**.
- Choose a pattern, then move the sliders to change the stars.
- Pick a colour set, or change any single colour.
- Turn off **Grout lines** to see that the tiles join with no seam.
- Press **Save in colour** or **Save black and white outline** (top of the page, or under the preview) to download one tile as a PNG.
